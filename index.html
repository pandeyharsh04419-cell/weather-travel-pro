🛣️ Full Route Weather Monitor

Create a route between two cities. The app samples intermediate points along the straight-line corridor and reports temperature, rain probability, wind, weather condition and a travel-risk indicator. The map remains available for the actual road route.

How route monitoring works

The route is divided into multiple weather checkpoints between the origin and destination. Each checkpoint is queried from the weather service. This gives a weather corridor view rather than only checking the two endpoints.
